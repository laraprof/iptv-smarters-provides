## 🔬 Our Testing Methodology: How We Ranked Them

Unlike other review sites that simply recycle lists, we built a genuine testing framework to ensure our recommendations are accurate. To compete with the very best out there, we knew we had to go deeper. Here is exactly how we evaluated over 30 different IPTV services over a 60-day period:

1. **Peak-Hour Stress Testing**: We tested each service during high-traffic Canadian events, specifically Saturday night NHL games (Hockey Night in Canada) and major UFC pay-per-views. A service that buffers when the Leafs play is a service we won't recommend.
2. **The "Canadian Content" Audit**: We manually verified the presence, quality, and stability of critical Canadian networks: CBC, CTV, Global, Citytv, TSN (1-5), and Sportsnet (Ontario, East, West, Pacific, ONE, and 360).
3. **Latency and Zapping Speed**: We measured the time it takes to switch between channels (zapping speed). Anything over 3 seconds was penalized. 
4. **Customer Support Ghost Test**: We sent anonymous support tickets to each provider on a Sunday afternoon reporting a fake "login issue" to measure response times and helpfulness.
5. **Device Compatibility**: We tested streams across Amazon Firestick 4K, NVIDIA Shield, Apple TV 4K, and Android mobile devices using popular apps like IPTV Smarters Pro and TiviMate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What internet speed do I need for IPTV in Canada?**
We recommend an absolute minimum of 30 Mbps for stable 1080p streaming, and 50+ Mbps if you intend to watch 4K sports without buffering. Always use an Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi when possible.

**Do I need a VPN for IPTV in Canada?**
While not strictly required by all providers, we highly recommend using a VPN (like NordVPN or ExpressVPN). Canadian ISPs like Bell and Rogers are known to occasionally throttle IPTV traffic during major sporting events. A VPN masks your traffic, preventing this throttling and ensuring a smooth stream.

**Can I watch on multiple TVs?**
Yes, but you usually need to purchase a multi-connection plan. Using a single-connection subscription on two devices simultaneously will usually result in your account being temporarily banned or caught in a buffering loop.
