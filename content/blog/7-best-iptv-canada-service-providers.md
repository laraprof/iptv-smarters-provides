## 2. CatchON TV
**Great for VOD lovers and movie buffs.**

CatchON TV has built a solid reputation over the years. Their live TV section is decent, but where they truly shine is their massive Video on Demand library. If you care more about replacing Netflix and Disney+ than watching live sports, this is a strong contender. 

**Pros:**
- Massive, frequently updated VOD section.
- User-friendly custom app.
**Cons:**
- Live sports can occasionally buffer during high-traffic events.
- Slightly more expensive than competitors.

## 3. Apollo Group TV
**A premium option with a slick interface.**

Apollo Group TV is one of the most well-known names in the IPTV space. They offer a highly polished app that feels very similar to traditional cable or Netflix. They have excellent USA coverage, though their Canadian local channel list isn't quite as comprehensive as our top pick.

**Pros:**
- Beautiful, intuitive interface.
- Excellent 4K movie selection.
**Cons:**
- Bitcoin/Crypto is often the only accepted payment method.
- Lacking some regional Canadian news networks.

## 4. Xtreme HD IPTV
**Best for international channels.**

If you are looking for content outside of North America, Xtreme HD is a fantastic choice. They boast over 20,000 channels globally, covering everything from European football to Asian dramas. 

**Pros:**
- Massive international channel list.
- Supports multiple connections simultaneously (for an extra fee).
**Cons:**
- The sheer volume of channels makes the EPG hard to navigate.
- Support response times can be slow.

## 5. Mom IPTV
**A budget-friendly option for casual viewers.**

Mom IPTV focuses on providing the basics at a very low price point. If you only watch TV occasionally and aren't overly concerned with 4K sports streams, this is a viable budget option.

**Pros:**
- Very affordable long-term plans.
- Decent selection of standard-definition channels.
**Cons:**
- Buffering is common during peak weekend hours.
- Customer support is practically non-existent.

## 6. Falcon TV
**Solid multi-device support.**

Falcon TV offers a reliable service with a focus on cross-platform compatibility. Whether you use a Firestick, Apple TV, or an Android smartphone, they have a setup guide for you.

**Pros:**
- Good device compatibility.
- Reliable standard definition streaming.
**Cons:**
- EPG (TV Guide) is frequently inaccurate or missing data.
- Missing many French-Canadian networks.

## 7. IPTV Trends
**A reliable backup provider.**

IPTV Trends rounds out our list. They offer a stable service with a good mix of USA, UK, and Canadian channels. While they don't excel in any one specific area, they are a solid all-rounder.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**
- The VOD library is rarely updated with new releases.
- No free trial available (paid trials only).
