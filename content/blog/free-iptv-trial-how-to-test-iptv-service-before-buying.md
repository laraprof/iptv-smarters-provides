## The Ultimate IPTV Trial Checklist: What to Test

Once you have your trial login and have set up your app, don't just put on a random channel and assume it's good. Run through this checklist to ensure the service is worth your money.

### 1. Test During Peak Hours
The true test of any IPTV server is how it handles heavy traffic. Test the service between **7:00 PM and 10:00 PM**, or during a major live sporting event (like a UFC PPV or an NFL Sunday). If it buffers excessively during these times, move on to another provider.

### 2. Check the Electronic Program Guide (EPG)
A live TV service is frustrating to use without a working TV guide. Browse through the channels and see if the EPG populates correctly. Does it show what's currently playing and what's coming up next?
[Learn more about how IPTV EPGs work →](https://iptvsmartproviders.com/blog/iptv-epg-explained-2025-best-iptv-canada-services-tv-guide)

### 3. Verify VOD (Video on Demand) Quality
Most premium subscriptions include a massive library of movies and TV series.
- Pick an older movie and a brand new release. 
- Fast-forward and rewind to see how the server handles seeking.
- Check if subtitles and multiple audio tracks are available.

### 4. Search for Your "Must-Have" Channels
Everyone has a few specific channels they can't live without—whether it's regional Canadian news (CBC, Global, CTV), a specific international network, or dedicated sports channels. Find them immediately and verify that the links are live and not broken.

### 5. Test Channel Switching Speed (Zapping)
How long does it take to switch from one channel to another? A high-quality provider will load the new channel in 1 to 3 seconds. Anything longer than 5 seconds can become very annoying over time.

## Red Flags to Watch Out For During Your Trial

If you experience any of the following during your 24-hour test, you should strongly reconsider buying a subscription:

- **Constant Buffering:** A little buffer here and there is normal (due to internet fluctuations), but if it freezes every 5 minutes, the provider's servers are overloaded.
- **Audio/Video Sync Issues:** The lips are moving, but the audio is a second behind.
- **Missing Categories:** The provider promised 20,000 channels, but whole categories (like USA Sports or UK Entertainment) are empty or broken.
- **No Customer Support:** If you have trouble setting up your trial and the provider's support team ignores your emails or WhatsApp messages, do not give them your money.
