## What Do You Actually Get With an IPTV Subscription?

Unlike traditional cable where you pay extra for every single "premium" channel package, a high-quality IPTV subscription usually operates on an all-inclusive model. A standard premium package should include:

1. **Global Live TV:** Tens of thousands of live channels from North America, Europe, Asia, and beyond.
2. **Premium Sports Packages:** Access to NFL Sunday Ticket, NBA League Pass, NHL Center Ice, MLB Extra Innings, and all major PPV events (UFC, Boxing, WWE).
3. **Massive VOD Library:** A constantly updated Video on Demand section featuring the latest cinema releases and popular TV series from major streaming networks.
4. **Electronic Program Guide (EPG):** A fully functioning TV guide so you can see what is currently airing and what is coming up next.

## Avoiding Scams: What to Watch Out For

Because the IPTV market is unregulated, there are many unreliable providers. Before buying a 12-month IPTV subscription, watch out for these massive red flags:

### 1. The "Too Good To Be True" Price
If a provider is offering a full year for $20 or $30, stay away. Maintaining load-balanced servers, anti-freeze technology, and high-quality 4K streams is incredibly expensive. Providers offering basement-bottom prices cram thousands of users onto cheap servers. The result? Your stream will buffer constantly, especially during high-traffic weekend sports events.

### 2. No Trial Available
Never buy an annual IPTV subscription without testing it first. Legitimate, confident providers will always offer a short trial (usually 24 hours) so you can test the channel quality on your own internet connection and device. 

### 3. Missing Customer Support
If you have an issue setting up your app or a channel goes down, you need help fast. If a provider only offers a generic contact form on a broken website, you are on your own. Look for providers that offer live chat or WhatsApp support.

## Month-to-Month vs. Annual Plans

When purchasing an IPTV subscription, you will usually be presented with options ranging from 1 month to 12 months. 

We always recommend starting with a **1-month or 3-month plan**. This allows you to thoroughly test the service's stability over a longer period. Once you are confident that the service doesn't buffer during peak hours and has all the channels you need, you can upgrade to a 6-month or 12-month plan to take advantage of the bulk discounts.
