## 🔧 How to Set Up IPTV on Apple TV (Step by Step)

### What You Need ✅

- [ ] Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, or Apple TV 4th gen
- [ ] Apple ID (for App Store)
- [ ] Internet connection (25 Mbps+ for HD, 50 Mbps+ for 4K)
- [ ] Active IPTV subscription (M3U URL or Xtream Codes)
- [ ] 5–10 minutes

### Method 1: IPTV Smarters Pro (Recommended)

**Step 1: Open App Store** — On your Apple TV home screen, open the App Store using your Siri Remote.

**Step 2: Search for IPTV Smarters** — Type "IPTV Smarters" in the search bar and download the app (it's free to install).

**Step 3: Launch the App** — Open IPTV Smarters Pro from your Apple TV home screen.

**Step 4: Add Your Subscription** — Select "Add User" → choose "Xtream Codes API" if your provider gave you username/password/URL, or "M3U URL" if you received a playlist link.

**Step 5: Load Your Channels** — Wait 30–90 seconds for the full channel list and EPG to load.

**Step 6: Navigate with Siri Remote** — Use the trackpad for navigation. Swipe left/right in the EPG to browse the TV schedule. Hold the microphone button and say a channel name to search instantly.

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** On Apple TV 4K, go to **Settings → Video and Audio → Format** and set to **4K HDR** or **4K Dolby Vision** before launching your IPTV app. This ensures every 4K channel plays at maximum quality.

### Method 2: AirPlay from iPhone (No Setup)

If you use IPTV Smarters Pro on your iPhone, you can instantly cast to Apple TV using **AirPlay** — no Apple TV configuration required.

1. Start playing a channel in IPTV Smarters Pro on your iPhone
2. Tap the **AirPlay icon** in the player
3. Select your **Apple TV** from the list
4. The stream immediately appears on your TV in full HD

## ❓ FAQ — IPTV on Apple TV

### Can I install IPTV apps directly on Apple TV?

**Yes.** The tvOS App Store includes multiple IPTV apps including IPTV Smarters Pro, Flex IPTV, and GSE Smart IPTV. No sideloading or developer certificates needed.

### Does Apple TV support 4K IPTV?

**Yes.** Apple TV 4K (both 2nd and 3rd generation) supports 4K HDR, Dolby Vision, and Dolby Atmos for premium IPTV content. This is the best 4K IPTV experience available on any device.

### Can I use Siri to control IPTV on Apple TV?

**Yes, partially.** Siri can open your IPTV app and control basic playback. Within IPTV Smarters Pro, the built-in search function lets you find channels by voice using the Siri Remote microphone button.

### Is IPTV on Apple TV legal?

**Yes.** Using licensed IPTV apps on Apple TV is completely legal. All apps listed in this guide are available on the official tvOS App Store and operate within Apple's app guidelines.

### How do I fix buffering on Apple TV with IPTV?

Apple TV has excellent hardware, so buffering usually indicates a network issue. Check your Wi-Fi signal strength, or connect via ethernet using a USB-C ethernet adapter. Ensure your internet speed is at least 25 Mbps.

### Can I use one IPTV subscription on Apple TV and iPhone?

**Yes.** Most IPTV subscriptions allow 2–4 simultaneous connections. Log into the same account on IPTV Smarters Pro on your Apple TV and iPhone simultaneously.
